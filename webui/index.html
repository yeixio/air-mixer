Universal Audio Protocol Translator & Mixer

How it works: Add input servers to receive audio, create routes to connect sources to destinations, and add output clients to send audio to your speakers. Audio flows from inputs through routes to outputs.

Input Servers

What are input servers?
Input servers receive audio from sources on your network. An AirPlay server shows up as an AirPlay speaker that your iPhone, iPad or Mac can play to. What it receives can be routed to your output clients.

What are output clients?
Output clients are where audio plays: the speakers and audio devices on your network. Add one for each speaker you want to control, then connect input servers to them with a route.

Audio Routes

What are routes?
Routes connect input servers to output clients. A route sends what one source receives, such as an AirPlay stream, to one or more speakers. Several routes can play into the same speaker. A route plays only while it's on.
